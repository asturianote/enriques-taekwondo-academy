Programs

Every black belt
starts with day one.

Classes are grouped by age and belt, so every student trains with peers at the same level. Beginners are welcome in every age group.

Clear steps from first class to black belt.

Ages 4–6

Little Warriors

Big energy, focused fun. Little Warriors build listening, coordination and self-control through games, basic kicks and simple routines, in a class made for young children.

Beyond class

Teams, private lessons and camps.

For students who want more. Teams launch as students qualify, by invitation from Master Suarez.

Demonstration Team

Aerial kicks, acrobatics and board breaking performed at community events. Tuesday and Thursday.

Poomsae Team

Forms training for precision, power and competition. Wednesday.

Sparring Team

Olympic-style sparring with proper protective gear and close supervision. Friday.

Private Classes

One-on-one or small-group lessons for focused goals and extra practice. Weekdays at 3:15 PM.

Holiday training camps. Camps during school holidays keep students active, learning and having fun while school is out.

Belt testing. Students test when they meet the requirements for their rank: forms, one-step sparring, board breaking, fitness and Korean terms.
