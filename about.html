About

Master Enrique Suarez

Kukkiwon 4th Degree Black Belt. Founder and head instructor of Eclipse Taekwondo.

His story

Ten years of building students and teams

Master Enrique has taught Taekwondo for more than ten years, to students from four-year-olds in their first class to adults in their seventies. He started as an instructor at Yong-In Tiger Taekwondo in 2015 and became its Head Instructor in 2020. For six years he led every class in a program of more than 200 active students across all ages and skill levels.

Along the way he built the coaching team, growing the assistant-instructor group from two to nine, and coached the Demonstration and Sparring Teams. He has led groups of 20 to 100 competitors at competitions and championships around South Florida.

Eclipse Taekwondo is his own school: small classes, honest membership, and the traditional art taught with care.
