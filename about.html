Meet Master
Enrique Suarez.

Master Suarez has trained in Taekwondo for 17 years and taught for more than 10. He has taught students from age 4 to 70+, coached demonstration and sparring teams, and prepared students for competitions across South Florida.

“Our mission is to build confidence, discipline and physical fitness in children, teens and adults through traditional Taekwondo training, in a safe, structured, family-friendly environment.”

The Five Tenets

What every student learns.

The five tenets of Taekwondo guide every class, on the mat and at home.

01

Courtesy

Ye-ui

Respect for instructors, classmates, family and ourselves.

02

Integrity

Yeom-chi

Knowing right from wrong, and choosing right.

03

Perseverance

In-nae

Keep going when it gets hard. Every black belt was once a beginner.

04

Self-Control

Geuk-gi

Control of body, temper and words, inside and outside the dojang.

05

Indomitable Spirit

Baekjul-bulgul

Courage to stand up for what is right, and never give up.
