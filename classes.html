Little Tigers

Ages 4–6

Who it’s for

First light. First habits.

Little Tigers builds coordination, listening, and confidence in a safe, supportive class. Short drills. Clear praise. The first habits of courtesy and self-control.

Parents can watch. Progress is personal — small groups mean every child is coached, not parked on a line.

Schedule coming soon with our South Florida location.

Juniors

Ages 6–12

Who it’s for

School-age. Real technique.

Juniors train stances, kicks, forms, and the discipline to carry it off the mat. Fitness and character move together — perseverance in class, integrity everywhere else.

Personalized attention stays the standard. Students know what they need for the next belt. Tests are included in membership — no surprise fees.

Schedule coming soon with our South Florida location.

Teens & Adults

Ages 12+

Who it’s for

Train with purpose.

Teens and adults share a floor that expects effort. Beginners start at zero. Returning students drill like beginners again. Conditioning is real. Ego is optional.

Physical fitness, self-discipline, and personal growth — coached in small groups so you cannot hide, and would not want to.

Schedule coming soon with our South Florida location.

Advanced

Black belt path · teams

Who it’s for

Black belt, and beyond.

The Advanced / Black Belt path is for students ready to deepen the art. Same tenets. Higher standard. Master Enrique Suarez leads with more than ten years of teaching.

When you are ready, Eclipse also fields a Demo team, Sparring team, and Poomsae team.

Team tryouts and advanced hours will be posted with our location.
