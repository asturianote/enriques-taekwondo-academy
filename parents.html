Safety first

How we keep every class safe.

  • SafeSport training and background checks for every adult who works with students
  • Two adults present with minors, and classes in an open area visible from the parent waiting area
  • Waiver and emergency information on file before the first class, including trial classes
  • Photos and videos of students are shared only with parent consent
  • CPR, First Aid and AED certified instructor, first aid kit and emergency plan on site
  • Mats cleaned and disinfected every class day

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What age can my child start?

Children can start at age 4 in Little Warriors (ages 4–6). Older kids join the class for their belt level, and teens and adults (13+) have their own class.

What should we wear to the first class?

Comfortable workout clothes are fine. Students train barefoot on the mats. No uniform or equipment is needed to try a class.

Can parents watch?

Yes. Classes take place in an open training area that can be seen from the parent waiting area.

Can parents train too?

Yes. Join the Family Class to train with your child, or the Teen + Adult class on your own.

Is sparring safe for kids?

Sparring is introduced step by step, with controlled partner drills matched by age and rank, proper protective gear and close supervision.

How does belt testing work?

Students test when they meet the requirements for their rank: forms, one-step sparring, board breaking, fitness and Korean terms. Master Suarez lets each family know when a student is ready.

Are classes taught in Spanish?

Classes are taught in English and Spanish, so the whole family feels at home.

Where are you, and when do you open?

Eclipse Taekwondo is opening in Southwest Broward. The exact location and opening date will be announced soon. Join the Founding Families waitlist to hear first.
