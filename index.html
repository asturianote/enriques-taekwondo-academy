The academy

A serious art. A warm room.

Founded by Master Enrique Suarez — 4th Dan Kukkiwon, with more than ten years teaching — Eclipse Taekwondo is built for people who want real progress, not a crowded line.

We train in Broward County, South Florida. The exact location is still being chosen. When the doors open, you will walk into small group classes, clear coaching, and a membership that stays honest.

How we train

Small groups. Real eyes on you.

Personalized attention is the point. You will be coached, not lost in the back row. Technique, fitness, and character move together — courtesy on the floor, discipline off it.
